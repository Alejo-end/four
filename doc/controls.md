# mangler controls

GENERATED by `lua test/controls.lua` from the script's own encoder and key
tables. Do not edit by hand; regenerate after changing a page.

Everywhere: E1 selects the page, K1 is shift, K1+E1 picks the track (the
scene morph is on SCENE's E3), K1+K2 is STOP, and K1+K3 is DRY on a tap or erases the page's
material when held for about a second.

Compound targets: `@win_move` slides the take window, `@lay_move` slides the
selected layer's loop, `@lay_level` and `@lay_rate` set the selected layer's
level and rate. `@mo_sel` walks MOTION's rows: the lanes, then the two
modulators; with a modulator selected the other knobs are its own.

Tracks (mangler2): four engine tracks and one focus. On TRACKS, E2 picks the
focused track and E3 sets its level; K2 turns it on and off, K3 cycles its
mode. Hold K1 there for its sends: K1+E2 picks the track it sends to, K1+E3
how much. TAKE, VOICE and FILTER then edit the focused track (TAKE 2, VOICE 2
...), with the same knobs reaching its `t<n>_` params; on TAKE 2 to 4, SNAP puts
the window on track 1's and back. K3 held on VOICE or FILTER also steps the
focus. Tracks 2 to 4 have no erosion: VOICE in PLAY keeps scan and pitch on
K1 and FRZ on K2 there. Drive and dry/wet are shared by every track.
