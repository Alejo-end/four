# mangler controls

GENERATED by `lua test/controls.lua` from the script's own encoder and key
tables. Do not edit by hand; regenerate after changing a page.

Everywhere: E1 selects the page, K1 is shift, K1+E1 morphs toward the selected
scene, K1+K2 is STOP, and K1+K3 is DRY on a tap or erases the page's
material when held for about a second.

Compound targets: `@win_move` slides the take window, `@lay_move` slides the
selected layer's loop, `@lay_level` and `@lay_rate` set the selected layer's
level and rate. `@mo_sel` walks MOTION's rows: the lanes, then the two
modulators; with a modulator selected the other knobs are its own.

Track 2 (mangler2): on VOICE and FILTER, K3 held to the arc switches the page
between track 1 and track 2 (`track_sel`), and the title reads VOICE 2 or
FILTER 2. On track 2 every knob and key below reaches the `t2_` twin of its
param. Track 2 has no erosion, so VOICE in PLAY keeps scan and pitch on K1
and FRZ on K2. Drive and dry/wet are shared by both tracks.
