# four controls

GENERATED by `lua test/controls.lua` from the script's own encoder and key
tables. Do not edit it by hand. Run it again after you change a page.

Each knob cell is the word the footer shows at rest, then the param id.

Everywhere: E1 selects the page (twelve, it stops at TAKE and at SCENE). K1
is shift: while it is held the footer shows the K1 layer. K1+E1 picks the
track. K1+K2 is STOP. K1+K3 is DRY on a tap. Held for about a second, it
erases the page's material where the Erase column says yes (the footer
reads ERASE and draws an arc once a release can no longer be a tap).

Keys: a key marked * follows the punch law: a tap latches it, a hold works
only while the key is down. `hold` names what a key does when it is held
to the end of its arc. Released before the arc shows, it is a tap.
Released while the arc fills, it does nothing. SCENE's RCL recalls on a
tap and borrows the scene while held (PEEK). On the < cell a tap is the
way back. A key with no mark acts on the press.

SCENE is a crossfader with two ends: E2 picks A, LIVE (the sound you have
when the fade leaves 0) or a stored slot. E3 picks B, the slot K2 recalls
and K3 captures into. K1+E3 fades from A to B and back. The orbit's drift
and reach are in PARAMS (orbit).

Compound targets: `@win_move` slides the take window. `@trk_level` is the
focused track's level. `@route_to` and `@route_amt` pick and set one of the
focused track's sends. `@mo_sel` walks MOTION's rows: the recorded lanes,
then the four modulators. With a modulator selected the other knobs are
its own.

Tracks: four engine tracks and one focus. On TRACKS, E2 picks the focused
track and E3 sets its level. K2 turns it on and off, K3 cycles its mode.
Hold K1 there for its sends: K1+E2 picks where it sends (another track,
D the delay or R the reverb), K1+E3 how much. TAKE, VOICE, FILTER and
EFFECT then edit the focused track (their page pop reads 2 VOICE ...),
with the same knobs reaching its `t<n>_` params. K3 held on VOICE, FILTER
or EFFECT steps the focus to the next track. Each track has its own drive
and crush (FILTER, K1). Dry/wet (MIX, K1) is one for all tracks.

VOICE: the knobs follow the focused track's mode. FRZ holds GRAIN, PV and
CLOUD. In WARP, and in PLAY on tracks 2 to 4, it waits for one of those
modes, and VOICE draws HELD dim.
